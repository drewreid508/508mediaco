Questions

Before you book a call

Do you work outside Greenville / the Upstate?

Yes — creative shoots are easiest in the Upstate SC area, but ad management works with any trade business in the US. If you can send footage or host a shoot day, we can run it.

What if I've never run ads before?

That's most of who we work with. The audit and first project are built to get you a working campaign without you needing to learn Meta Ads Manager.

How fast until I see leads?

Campaigns typically go live within a week of the shoot. Lead flow depends on budget and season, but you'll see cost-per-lead numbers from week one.

Is there a contract?

Project work is one-time. Monthly retainers run a 3-month minimum, then month-to-month — enough time to actually judge results.
