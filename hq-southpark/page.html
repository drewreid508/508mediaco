Creative approach

A home you feel, not a walkthrough

We wanted South Park to feel cinematic and lifestyle-first, not like a room-by-room property tour. Mya opens the front door and talks to the viewer the way she would to a buyer, and the drone pulls back to show the whole community taking shape.

Location

The South Park model home in Piedmont, plus aerials of the lots and the homes going up around it.

Camera movement

Smooth handheld moves through the home with Mya, and drone shots for scale.

Transitions

Warm light leaks and film burns carry you from room to room.

Sound design

A music bed with hits timed to each cut and each on-screen moment.

Motion graphics

Square-footage counters, lot details and Mya's name card, animated to her voice.

Story

The close follows one lot from bare dirt at night to a finished home at daybreak.
