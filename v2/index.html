How booking works

Simple and personal. Every project gets its own quote, and nothing is booked until we've both agreed on it.

  1. Send a requestTell me about your project and pick a preferred date and a backup.
  2. I'll reach outI review every request myself and contact you to talk it through.
  3. Agree on a quoteYou get a custom quote. Nothing starts until you're happy with it.
  4. Confirm your dateA deposit invoice locks in the date. The balance follows the terms we agreed.
Submitting an inquiry does not automatically confirm or reserve your requested date. All bookings are subject to availability and confirmation.
