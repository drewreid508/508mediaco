How a partnership works

Planned ahead, so shoot days run smoothly.

  1. Proposal callWe talk about your business, your goals and what content would help most.
  2. Custom proposalYou get a written proposal with what's included each month.
  3. Plan and scheduleWe plan each month's content and put shoot dates on the calendar in advance.
  4. Shoot, edit, deliverI film, edit and deliver finished content ready to use.
Partnership terms

Clear expectations on both sides.

  • Three-month starting agreementPartnerships begin with three months, then continue month to month.
  • Invoiced monthlyAn invoice goes out at the start of each billing period. No automatic charges.
  • Shoot dates set in advanceIf a date needs to change, let me know as early as possible. New dates depend on availability.
  • Defined revisionsEach package includes a set number of revision rounds, agreed in your proposal.
  • Extra work, quoted firstAnything outside your package is quoted separately before it starts.
  • A limited number of partnersI only take on a few monthly clients at a time, so each one gets real attention.
