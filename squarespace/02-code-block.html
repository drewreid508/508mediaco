About

Behind the camera.

508 Media Co. is led by Drew Reid, owner and director of photography. Drew plans each shoot, runs the camera and edits the finished work.

He picked up a camera at eleven, filming motocross. Chasing riders around a track taught him to shoot fast and frame something moving before the moment's gone. Seven years later he built 508 Media on it.

Today that instinct goes into work for businesses, brands and vehicles: Reels, ad creative, automotive content, photography and drone. Get the shot, cut it so people watch, and deliver something a business can sell with.
