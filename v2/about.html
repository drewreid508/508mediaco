About

The person behind the camera.

Hey, I'm Drew.

Born and raised in Piedmont, South Carolina. I grew up on dirt bikes, and around 11 I saw a motocross edit on Instagram and said, "I want to do that." I've had a camera in my hands ever since.

Now I film for businesses and vehicle owners around the Upstate. My favorite part is taking an idea from a conversation and turning it into something people actually want to watch.

How I approach a project

Every project starts with a conversation. I want to understand your business or your vehicle, what you want the content to do and who it's for. Then I plan the shoot around that, so the day itself runs smoothly and nothing important gets missed.

I shoot and edit everything myself. That means the person you talked to on the first call is the same person making every decision about the final cut.

Why the final result matters to me

My name is on every piece I deliver. I'd rather take the time to get it right than hand over something that's just "good enough." When a client is proud to post what I made, that's the job done.

What working with me is like

Direct. No account managers or handoffs. You talk to me.

Honest. Clear quotes, clear expectations and straight answers. If an idea won't work, I'll tell you and suggest something that will.

Organized. Shoots are planned and scheduled ahead of time, and I show up prepared. And I'm not afraid to get my camera wet or dirty, whether that's hanging off the back of a truck or a boat.
